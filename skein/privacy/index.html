Skein Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Skein app for iPhone ("Skein", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Skein is published under the bundle identifier com.skein.knhjrz.

Skein helps short-stay hosts run guest stays: scheduling arrivals and departures, room preparation and reset checklists, voice memos with transcription, walkthrough photos and videos, a lost and found log, checklist protocol templates, and data exports.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at Skeincontact@gmail.com.

1. Information you create in Skein

Skein saves the information you enter or capture so that you can use the app's features. This includes:

  • Your host profile: the host name you choose and an optional profile photo.
  • Stays and schedules: stay titles, guest names you type in, rooms, planned and actual dates, time zones, team roster entries, calendar memos, door codes, Wi-Fi details and digital guide text that you enter.
  • Checklists and protocols: tasks, completion and skip history with reasons, and the protocol templates you create or duplicate.
  • Media: room photos, walkthrough videos and voice memos you capture or import, together with checksums recorded for them.
  • Voice memo transcripts: the editable text produced from your voice memos.
  • Lost and found records: item descriptions, photos and voice memos.
  • Activity: a list of recent changes shown on the Stays home screen and in related views.
  • Credits: your credit balance and purchase and spending history.
  • Export history: the file name, scope, size and checksum of archives you export.

This information is saved in Skein's app storage on your iPhone (Core Data database, app files and iOS data protection). We do not receive the records you create unless you choose to send them to us, for example by email.

1.1 Information about other people

Stays may contain names or details of your guests and team members that you type in yourself. You are responsible for having the right to record that information and for handling it in line with the laws that apply to you.

2. Entry and session information

When you tap Get Started on the entry page for the first time, Skein silently creates a random identifier for your use of the app. No name, email address, phone number or password is requested.

  • The identifier is stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • The session state and the date and time you accepted the Terms of Use are stored in the app's settings (UserDefaults).
  • End Session (Vault → Session) closes your session and returns you to the entry page. Your records are kept, and you continue with them the next time you tap Get Started.

3. Permissions and how they are used

Skein asks for each permission through the standard iOS dialog only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change any permission at any time in iOS Settings. If you decline, Skein keeps what you were working on and offers another way to continue.

Permission Why Skein asks When it is requested
Camera To capture room photos and walkthrough videos for your stay records and lost and found items. The first time you take a photo or record a video.
Microphone To record voice memos and the audio track of walkthrough videos. The first time you record a voice memo or a video.
Speech Recognition To turn your voice memos into editable text notes. The first time you transcribe a voice memo.
Photo Library (read) To let you pick photos and videos from your library to attach to a stay, inspection or lost and found record. The first time you import from your library.
Photo Library (add) To save inspection photos and videos to your Photos when you choose Save to Photos. The first time you use Save to Photos.
Notifications Skein sends notifications related to the features you use in the app, according to how the app works. The first time the Stays screen appears.

You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

3.1 Speech transcription

Transcription uses Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your device, language and settings, Apple may process the audio of the voice memo you choose to transcribe in order to return the text. That processing is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The resulting transcript is saved with your note in Skein and can be edited or deleted.

4. Facial data

4.1 What is collected

Skein does not collect, create or use facial geometry, face prints, Face ID data or any other biometric identifiers. Skein does not run face detection or face recognition on any photo or video.

Photos and videos that you choose to capture or import (for example room walkthroughs, lost and found items, or your optional profile photo) may incidentally show a person's face. Skein treats these only as ordinary images and video.

4.2 How it is used

Such images and videos are used only to show them back to you inside the feature where you added them (stay records, inspection media, lost and found, or your profile photo), and in exports you create. No facial analysis is performed.

4.3 Sharing and where it is stored

Images and videos are saved in Skein's app storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party. They leave the app only when you choose to share them yourself, for example through Save to Photos, an exported archive shared through the iOS share sheet, or an email you write.

4.4 Retention

Images and videos are kept until you delete them (for example with Delete on the item), until you use Delete All Data, or until you delete the app. Originals in your Photos library are never changed by Skein.

4.5 Purpose of this section

This Section 4 is our explanation of how any facial data (including images that may depict a face) is collected, used, disclosed, shared and retained by Skein.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple: in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and speech transcription uses Apple's Speech framework (see Section 3.1).
  • No third-party processing services: Skein does not send your text, photos, videos or audio to third-party services.
  • No advertising or analytics: Skein contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Open-source components: Skein includes open-source libraries for layout, on-screen messages, keyboard handling and creating ZIP files. These components run inside the app and do not send your data anywhere.

6. In-app purchases and credits

Skein offers consumable credit packs through Apple's in-app purchase system. Credits are spent on exporting an archive package (1 credit) and on creating or duplicating a protocol template (1 credit each, after your first 2 free templates).

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Skein stores the transaction identifier, product, credits and date so that each purchase is credited once. Credits are stored on your device and are removed if you delete the app or use Delete All Data.

7. Contact and support

Contact Developer (Vault → Safety) opens an email draft addressed to the developer. Contact information: Skeincontact@gmail.com.

We use emails you send only to respond to your request and to improve Skein.

8. Exports

Export creates a ZIP archive with JSON records, CSV summaries and, if you choose, inspection media, along with a checksum. The archive is shared through the iOS share sheet to a destination you pick (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). Once you share it, the recipient or service you choose handles it under its own terms.

9. Delete All Data

Delete All Data (Vault → Your Data) asks for confirmation and then permanently removes from the app:

  • every stay, schedule, checklist, task history, protocol template, lost and found record and activity entry you have;
  • all photos, videos and voice memos stored by Skein, and export files;
  • your host profile and profile photo;
  • your credit balance and purchase history;
  • your Keychain identifier, session state and Terms acceptance.

After deletion you return to the entry page. Content provided with the app is shown again as on a fresh install. Items you saved to your Photos library or shared elsewhere are not affected. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.

10. Data security

Skein uses iOS data protection for its files and stores the session identifier in the iOS Keychain. No method of storage is completely secure, so please protect your device with a passcode.

11. Data sale and sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

12. Children's privacy

Skein is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export or delete your information at any time inside Skein, change permissions in iOS Settings, and use Delete All Data to remove everything. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights; contact us to exercise them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of Skein after an update means you accept the revised policy.
