3. What Skein does

Skein is a hospitality operations app for short-stay hosts. It lets you:

  • schedule stays, arrivals, departures and turnovers on a calendar with a team roster;
  • run room preparation and reset checklists based on protocol templates, with completion, skip and reopen history;
  • record voice memos and turn them into editable text with Apple Speech;
  • capture walkthrough photos and short videos and review them in an inspection media gallery;
  • log lost and found items;
  • keep door codes, Wi-Fi QR codes and a digital guide for guests;
  • search your archive and export records as a ZIP archive with checksums;
  • view and share Moments with hosts in the Skein community.
